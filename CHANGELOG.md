# Mobile - Changelog

## v1.32 - 2026-11-19
- Photos kept only in cloud storage no longer show on the phone, where they could not be opened.

## v1.31 - 2026-11-19
- Recent photos for the phone app's Photos tab, the last imported first.
- Photo lists carry stars, Reject and edited, so the phone app's grid can show them.
- The phone app's tags page offers your recent tags.

## v1.30 - 2026-11-18
- Photos saved or downloaded on the phone are the photo as PhotoDewey shows it: with your edits, at full size, keeping the capture date.

## v1.29 - 2026-11-18
- The phone app can show statistics for your library: photos, size, folders, albums, tags, people and more. The phone can switch PhotoDewey on the PC to another of your libraries. The phone stays connected when the PC switches library.

## v1.28 - 2026-11-18
- The phone can now change exposure and contrast, restore faded colours and crop photos.
- In the phone's gallery, the new toolbar applies these to several photos at once.

## v1.27 - 2026-11-17
- Change photos from your phone: add and remove tags, set stars, flag for deletion, rotate, Auto Optimize and add to an album, for one photo or a selection, and undo the phone's last change. Needs PhotoDewey with plug-in API 1.1.

## v1.26 - 2026-11-17
- The phone's photo info shows exposure values as you would read them (3.8 mm, f/2.2, 1/17 s).

## v1.25 - 2026-11-17
- Folders on the phone are shown as a tree, as on the PC, with Newly Imported at the top.
- Make new folders from the phone.
- The + button shows only in Folders and in an open folder, and uploads into the open folder.

## v1.24 - 2026-11-17
- Thumbnails on the phone now update when a photo is edited on the PC.

## v1.23 - 2026-11-17
- Phone and PC now prove to each other that they know the pairing code before anything is shared.
- Add several photos at once from the phone, choose the folder and add tags.
- Photos from the phone get their date, camera and place like imported photos, and show on the PC at once.
- On Android, share photos to PhotoDewey from the gallery's Share menu.
- Uploads over slow connections no longer fail, and only supported file types are taken.
- After updating, restart PhotoDewey on the PC.

## v1.22 - 2026-11-16
- Regenerating the pairing code now takes effect immediately. Photos uploaded from the phone no longer overwrite a file with the same name. Added a user manual.

## v1.21 - 2026-11-16
- The photo on screen is sent before prefetched photos, so swiping in full screen starts loading at once.
- Downloads the phone no longer needs are stopped.
- The phone shows when your PC is creating the image and how fast the photo arrives.
- The phone notices a lost connection within seconds and reconnects.

## v1.20 - 2026-11-16
- Smart albums now open on the phone and show their photos.
- The album list marks smart albums with their own icon.
- Full screen on the phone shows a sharper copy of the photo, ready for zooming.
- The phone can show a photo's EXIF and IPTC metadata and its list of edits.

## v1.19 - 2026-11-16
- Faster, more reliable connection to the phone app.
- Thumbnails and photos load faster, straight from the library's thumbnail cache.
- Photo details now show the title, description, place, file type and author correctly.
- More reliable photo transfers to the phone.
