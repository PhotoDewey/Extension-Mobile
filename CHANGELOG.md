# Mobile - Changelog

## v1.22 - 2026-11-16
- Regenerating the pairing code now takes effect immediately. Photos uploaded from the phone no longer overwrite a file with the same name. Added a user manual.

## v1.21 - 2026-11-16
- The photo on screen is sent before prefetched photos, so swiping in full screen starts loading at once.
- Downloads the phone no longer needs are stopped.
- The phone shows when your PC is creating the image and how fast the photo arrives.
- The phone notices a lost connection within seconds and reconnects.

## v1.20 - 2026-11-16
- Smart albums now open on the phone and show their photos.
- The album list marks smart albums with their own icon.
- Full screen on the phone shows a sharper copy of the photo, ready for zooming.
- The phone can show a photo's EXIF and IPTC metadata and its list of edits.

## v1.19 - 2026-11-16
- Faster, more reliable connection to the phone app.
- Thumbnails and photos load faster, straight from the library's thumbnail cache.
- Photo details now show the title, description, place, file type and author correctly.
- More reliable photo transfers to the phone.
